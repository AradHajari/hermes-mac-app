Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Hermes Mac is used only by its owner, Arad Hajari, on his own computer. It accesses only the Google account that Arad himself authorizes.

  • What it accesses: the Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts data Arad grants, only to carry out his requests.
  • Where data goes: data is processed on Arad's Mac. To answer a request, relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider Arad has configured. It is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train models by Hermes Mac.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is stored locally on Arad's Mac. No data is stored on any server operated for Hermes Mac.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes Mac's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
